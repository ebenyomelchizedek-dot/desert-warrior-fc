About Desert Warriors FC

More than a football club — we are a community built on talent, discipline, teamwork and the passion for football.

OUR STORY

The Journey of Desert Warriors FC

Desert Warriors Football Club was founded in April 2020 with the aim of nurturing football talent, promoting sportsmanship and providing a platform for young players to develop professionally.

The club was formed through the collective efforts of community leaders, sports enthusiasts and former players who shared a vision of using football as a tool for youth empowerment, discipline and social development.

Since its establishment, Desert Warriors FC has continued to build a strong foundation focused on professionalism, sustainability and competitive excellence.
