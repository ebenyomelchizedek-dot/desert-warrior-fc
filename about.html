OUR STORY

Desert Warriors Football Club (DW FC) was founded in April 2020 in Napetet, Lodwar, Kenya.

The club was established with the aim of nurturing football talent, promoting sportsmanship, and providing a platform for young players to develop professionally.

Since its establishment, the club has grown steadily, participating in local, regional and national competitions under the Football Kenya Federation (FKF).

OUR VISION & MISSION

OUR VISION
