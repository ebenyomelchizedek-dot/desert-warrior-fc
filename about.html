OUR BEGINNING

From a shared dream to a football movement.

Turkana Desert Warriors Football Club was founded in April 2020 with the aim of nurturing football talent, promoting sportsmanship and providing a platform for young players to develop their abilities.

The club was formed through the collective efforts of community leaders, sports enthusiasts and former players who shared a vision of using football as a tool for youth empowerment, discipline and social development.

Since its establishment, Turkana Desert Warriors FC has continued to build a strong foundation focused on professionalism, sustainability and competitive excellence.

Our ambition is to grow beyond the football pitch and become an institution that creates opportunities, inspires young people and represents our community with pride.
