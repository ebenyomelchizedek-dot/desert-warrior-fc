1. Introduction

Desert Warriors Football Club respects the privacy of its supporters, players, partners, visitors and other individuals who interact with the club.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect personal information when you visit our website or communicate with us.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the club, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your email address and telephone number.
  • Messages, enquiries or feedback you send to us.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when communicating with the club.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through general website forms unless it is necessary and requested through an appropriate process.

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect information when you:

  • Contact us through our website or email.
  • Submit a message, enquiry or feedback form.
  • Communicate with the club through its official channels.
  • Voluntarily provide information during club activities.

Website contact forms may use third-party services to deliver messages to the club. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information received may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and enquiries.
  • Communicate important club information.
  • Manage relationships with supporters and partners.
  • Improve our website and communication services.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing Information

Desert Warriors FC does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared where necessary with service providers helping us operate our website or communication services, where required by law, or where you have given appropriate permission.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining relevant club records and meeting legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, ask for corrections, request deletion where appropriate, or raise concerns about how your information is handled.

You can contact the club using the details provided below to make a privacy-related request.

9. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites, including social media platforms and partner websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external services.

We encourage visitors to read the privacy policies of external websites they visit.

10. Policy Updates

Desert Warriors FC may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our activities, website services or applicable requirements.

Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
