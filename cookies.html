1. Introduction

Desert Warriors Football Club is committed to providing visitors with a reliable and user-friendly website. This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit our website.

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a visitor's device, such as a computer, tablet or mobile phone.

They can help websites remember certain information, maintain functionality and understand how visitors interact with website pages.

3. Why We Use Cookies

Depending on the services and features enabled on our website, cookies may be used to:

  • Support essential website functionality.
  • Remember certain visitor preferences.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Understand website traffic and visitor activity, where analytics tools are enabled.
  • Support services provided by trusted third parties.

4. Types of Cookies

Websites commonly use different categories of cookies. The categories below explain their general purposes; they do not confirm that every category is currently active on the Desert Warriors FC website.

Essential Cookies

These support basic website functions and features necessary for a website or service to operate.

Preference Cookies

These may remember choices such as language or display preferences to provide a more convenient browsing experience.

Analytics Cookies

Where analytics services are enabled, these cookies may help website owners understand visitor numbers, popular pages and general website usage.

Third-Party Cookies

These may be placed by external services integrated into a website, subject to the services being used.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Our website may contain links, embedded content or features provided by third-party services, including social media platforms and external websites.

Those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Desert Warriors FC does not control the cookie practices of external services.

Visitors should review the privacy and cookie policies of those providers for further information.

6. Managing Cookies

Visitors can manage or restrict cookies through the privacy or security settings of their web browser.

Most browsers allow users to:

  • View cookies stored on their devices.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block some or all cookies.
  • Receive notifications when cookies are being used.

The steps for managing cookies differ between browsers. Please consult your browser's official support information for instructions.

7. Disabling Cookies

Disabling certain cookies may affect how some websites operate. Depending on the features being used, some functions or services may not work as intended.

Essential website functions may also depend on technologies that cannot be disabled without affecting the service.

8. Privacy and Personal Data

Information collected through cookies or similar technologies may, in some circumstances, relate to an identifiable visitor.

Where personal information is processed, Desert Warriors FC will handle it in accordance with applicable data protection requirements and our Privacy Policy.

9. Policy Updates

Desert Warriors FC may revise this Cookie Policy when website features, technologies or applicable requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
