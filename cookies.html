Your Privacy Matters to Us

This policy explains what cookies are, why websites use them, and how you can manage your preferences when visiting the Desert Warriors FC website. We aim to make your online experience convenient while respecting your privacy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them. They can help websites remember certain information, such as preferences, settings, or activity during a browsing session.

Cookies may help a website operate properly, improve navigation, and provide a more consistent experience when you return.

2. Why Cookies May Be Used

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support:

⚙️

Essential Cookies

Support important website functions, security, navigation, and features that visitors request.

🛠️

Preference Cookies

Remember choices such as website preferences or settings to make future visits more convenient.

📊

Analytics Cookies

If enabled, may help us understand how visitors interact with pages so the website can be improved.

📣

Third-Party Cookies

Certain embedded or external services may use their own cookies subject to their privacy policies.

3. Cookies and Website Services

Some features on our website, including supporter account services or embedded third-party content, may rely on technologies that store or access information on your device.

The technologies used depend on the features currently active on the website. We do not claim to use every cookie category listed in this policy.

4. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can use your browser settings to block, delete, or restrict cookies. Most browsers provide privacy controls for managing stored website information.

  • Block cookies from selected websites.
  • Delete cookies already stored on your device.
  • Choose whether websites can save certain preferences.
  • Use private browsing where available.

Blocking certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some website features.
