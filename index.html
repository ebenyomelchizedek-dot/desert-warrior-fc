DWFC

A Club With A Purpose

Founded in April 2020, Turkana Desert Warriors Football Club was established to nurture football talent, promote sportsmanship and create opportunities for young players to develop.

From Napetet in Lodwar, the club continues to build a strong foundation focused on professionalism, teamwork, youth development and positive community impact.
