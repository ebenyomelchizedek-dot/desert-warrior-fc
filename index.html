OUR STORY

More Than Just Football

Turkana Desert Warriors Football Club was founded in 2020 with a vision of nurturing football talent, promoting sportsmanship and creating opportunities for young players to develop.

The club continues to build a strong foundation based on professionalism, discipline, teamwork and community development.

OFFICIAL COMMUNITY PARTNER

Kenya Red Cross Society Turkana Branch

Desert Warriors Football Club is proud to partner with Kenya Red Cross Society Turkana Branch. This collaboration reflects our shared commitment to community development, humanitarian values, social responsibility and positive impact. Together, we look forward to supporting community initiatives and using football to promote unity, hope and positive change across Turkana County.
