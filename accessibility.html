1. Our Commitment

Desert Warriors Football Club believes that everyone should have the opportunity to access information about the club, its activities and its community.

We are committed to improving the accessibility and usability of our website so that visitors, including people with disabilities, can access information as easily as possible.

2. Accessibility Approach

We aim to make our website clear, understandable and convenient to use across different devices and browsing environments.

Our accessibility approach is informed by recognised web accessibility principles, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

Our aim is to work towards a website that is perceivable, operable, understandable and robust.

3. Website Accessibility Features

We aim to provide useful accessibility features and design practices, including:

  • Clear headings and organised page content.
  • Readable text and consistent navigation.
  • Responsive layouts for computers, tablets and mobile phones.
  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images where provided.
  • Links with clear and understandable labels.
  • Appropriate colour contrast where practical.

4. Ongoing Improvements

Website accessibility is an ongoing process. Desert Warriors FC will continue reviewing its website and considering improvements as new content and features are introduced.

We aim to identify and address accessibility barriers as they become known.

5. Known Limitations

Some website content or features may not yet provide the best possible experience for every visitor or assistive technology.

Certain third-party services, embedded content or external websites may also have accessibility limitations outside our direct control.

We welcome feedback that helps us identify and improve these areas.

6. Accessibility Assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing any information on our website, you may contact Desert Warriors FC for assistance.

Where reasonably possible, we will endeavour to provide the requested information through an alternative communication method.

7. Feedback and Suggestions

We value feedback from our website visitors. If you notice an accessibility issue or have suggestions for improving our website, please let us know.

When reporting an issue, it is helpful to mention the page you were visiting and the difficulty you experienced.
