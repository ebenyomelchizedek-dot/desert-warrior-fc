OUR HOME

Where Our Journey Continues

Lodwar, Turkana County, Kenya

Since September 2020, Lodwar Mixed Primary Ground has served as the home training and match venue of Desert Warriors FC. It is an important part of the club's journey, providing a place where players prepare, compete and continue developing their football abilities.

OUR STORY

History of Our Home Ground

The journey of Desert Warriors FC at Lodwar Mixed Primary Ground.

September 2020 — A New Home

Desert Warriors FC began using Lodwar Mixed Primary Ground in September 2020. The club management held discussions with the school management to secure the playing field for the team's training sessions and football matches. This arrangement established the ground as an important home for the club.

Ground Preparation and Maintenance

The club has continued taking responsibility for preparing and maintaining the playing surface. Activities include levelling the ground and marking the pitch lines to make it ready for training and matches.

A Home for Training and Competition

Over the years, the ground has continued serving the club for its training sessions and football matches. The club reports no significant challenges in using the venue for these activities.

Today — Building for the Future

Lodwar Mixed Primary Ground remains an important part of Desert Warriors FC. Although dedicated club facilities have not yet been established, the club continues to use and maintain the ground while pursuing its long-term development ambitions.
