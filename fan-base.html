OUR STORY

From independent supporters to one united family.

The Desert Warriors FC Fan Base is built on passion, loyalty and a shared commitment to the growth of Desert Warriors Football Club.

Initially, supporters came together independently, each showing their commitment to the club in their own way. Their dedication demonstrated that Desert Warriors FC had a growing community of people who believed in its vision and future.

Early in the 2026/27 season, the club took an important step towards bringing these supporters together through a supporters' sponsorship initiative. The initiative introduced a sponsorship kit targeting 3,000 loyal fans willing to become part of the club's sponsorship docket.

3,000 SUPPORTERS. ONE CLUB. ONE DREAM.

The initiative represents a new chapter in our journey: transforming individual support into collective strength and creating a structured platform through which fans can contribute directly to the club's development.

For the 2026/27 season, the DWFC Fan Base proudly takes its place as the club's main sponsor, demonstrating that the greatest strength of a football club can come from the people who stand behind it.

OUR COMMITMENT TO ACCOUNTABILITY

Our supporters deserve to know how their money is being used.

Desert Warriors FC is committed to establishing a Fan Base Oversight Committee whose members will be elected or approved by registered supporters in accordance with the programme's governance arrangements.

The committee will be responsible for:

  • Reviewing and scrutinising the club's financial records.
  • Monitoring how supporters' contributions are received and utilised.
  • Approving agreed financial accountability reports.
  • Ensuring members receive regular financial updates.
  • Promoting transparency, accountability and responsible financial management.

Our vision is to build a sponsorship programme where every supporter feels confident, informed and proud of their contribution.
