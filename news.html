CLUB DEVELOPMENT

Management Meets Players Ahead of the New Season

26 August 2026 Erait Pension Restaurant

Desert Warriors FC held a general meeting with the club's players and management on 26 August 2026 at Erait Pension Restaurant. The meeting brought together the club's leadership and players for an important conversation about the future direction of the club.

New Management Unveiled

One of the major highlights of the meeting was the unveiling of the new Desert Warriors FC management team. The club also announced position changes for some management members following the previous season. These changes form part of the club's efforts to strengthen its leadership structure.

Players Share Their Expectations

The meeting provided players with time to interact directly with management and openly share their expectations. It gave the players an opportunity to express their views and ideas while allowing management to listen and better understand the needs of the squad.

Exploring Sponsors and Partnerships

Management also discussed possibilities for the club's future, including securing sponsors and establishing partnerships that could support Desert Warriors FC and contribute to its continued growth.

BUILDING THE FUTURE TOGETHER

The meeting provided an important platform for open communication between management and players. The club looks forward to working together with its players, supporters, potential sponsors and partners towards a stronger future.

The club appreciates the commitment of everyone who continues to believe in Desert Warriors FC and looks forward to an exciting new chapter.
