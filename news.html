Desert Warriors FC Management Meets Players Ahead of New Season

📅 26 August 2026 📍 Erait Pension Restaurant

Desert Warriors FC held a general meeting with the club's players and management on 26 August 2026 at Erait Pension Restaurant. The meeting brought together the club's leadership and players for an important conversation about the future direction of the club.

⚽
A New Chapter for Desert Warriors FC

The meeting marked an important step as the club introduced its new management structure and opened discussions with the players about the season ahead.

01

New Management Unveiled

One of the major highlights of the meeting was the unveiling of the new Desert Warriors FC management team.

The club also announced position changes for some management members following the previous season. The changes form part of the club's efforts to strengthen its leadership structure and create a stronger foundation for the season ahead.

02

Players Share Their Expectations

The meeting provided the players with enough time to interact directly with the management and openly share their expectations as players of Desert Warriors FC.

The conversation created an opportunity for the players to express their views, ideas and expectations while allowing the management to listen and better understand the needs of the squad.

03

Exploring Sponsors and Partnerships

Management also took the opportunity to highlight some of the possibilities being explored as the club looks towards the future.

Among the areas discussed was the possibility of securing sponsors and establishing partnerships that could support Desert Warriors FC and contribute to the continued growth of the club.

“

Together, the management and players can build a stronger foundation for the future of Desert Warriors FC.

LOOKING AHEAD

Building the Future Together

The meeting provided an important platform for open communication between the management and players. With the new management structure now introduced, Desert Warriors FC looks forward to working together with its players, supporters, potential sponsors and partners towards a stronger future.

The club appreciates the commitment of everyone who continues to believe in Desert Warriors FC and looks forward to an exciting new chapter.
